## Structured idea

**Credit Scoring for Gig Workers via UPI History**

- Industry: Finance / FinTech
- Problem: Gig workers often lack a formal credit record, making it difficult for them to access loans from traditional lenders. Lenders struggle to assess the repayment ability of this demographic without standard financial documentation.
- Solution: A credit scoring service that analyzes UPI transaction history with user consent to estimate repayment capacity. It provides lenders with a reliable score for individuals who are otherwise excluded from the formal credit system.
- Target customers: Gig workers in Indian cities; Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs); Lenders
- Key features: UPI transaction history analysis; Consent-based data access; Repayment ability estimation; Credit scoring for thin-file borrowers; Data privacy and security protocols
